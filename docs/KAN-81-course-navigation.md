# KAN-81 — learner course navigation refinement

This update changes only the pilot learner navigation experience.

## Course entry

Starting or reviewing **Flood Crisis Management in Estuarine Areas** from the public course page now opens the authenticated learner **course overview** at:

`/learner?course=flood-crisis-management-estuarine-areas`

The learner therefore sees the units/progress overview first instead of being sent directly to Unit 1.

## Course outline sidebar

Inside the pilot learner workspace, a persistent side outline lists the whole course:

- Unit 1 — three lessons + required knowledge check
- Unit 2 — one lesson + practice knowledge check
- Unit 3 — five lessons + scenario activity

Unit, lesson and assessment items are navigable. The active item is highlighted. On smaller screens the outline stacks above the content and becomes scrollable.

## Final-lesson navigation

The final lesson of Unit 1 and Unit 2 now exposes two choices:

- **Go to quiz**
- **Next Unit**

This allows a learner to continue to the next unit and return to the quiz later. The existing Unit 1 completion rule is unchanged: Unit 1 still cannot be marked complete until its required knowledge check is submitted.

The last Unit 3 lesson links to its scenario activity.

## Preserved content

The Unit 2 YouTube lecture, theory, three figures, downloadable DOCX and seven-question practice quiz remain unchanged. Unit 3 scenario content also remains unchanged.
