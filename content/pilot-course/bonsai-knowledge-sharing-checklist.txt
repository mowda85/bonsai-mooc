BONSAI Knowledge Sharing Checklist
Pilot course: Information Systems and Knowledge Dissemination
Unit 3 — Knowledge Dissemination in BONSAI

Use this prototype checklist before sharing a BONSAI project result or lesson learned.

[ ] 1. Define the audience.
Identify who needs the information: project partners, authorities, practitioners, learners, societal partners, or the public.

[ ] 2. State the purpose.
Explain what the audience should understand, decide, or do after receiving the information.

[ ] 3. Check the source and context.
Record where the data or evidence came from, what it represents, and any important limitations.

[ ] 4. Turn results into a clear message.
Summarise the main finding in plain language and explain why it matters for flood resilience or disaster management.

[ ] 5. Choose an appropriate format.
Match the format to the audience: short summary, visual, technical document, training material, course content, or presentation.

[ ] 6. Make the material reusable.
Use a clear title, date/version, responsible partner, and enough context for another BONSAI partner to understand the material later.

[ ] 7. Support transnational learning.
Where relevant, explain what partners in other regions or countries can learn from the result and what local differences should be considered.

[ ] 8. Review before dissemination.
Check accuracy, clarity, permissions, and whether the message is suitable for the intended audience.

Prototype note: This checklist is sample MOOC content, not an official BONSAI project procedure.
