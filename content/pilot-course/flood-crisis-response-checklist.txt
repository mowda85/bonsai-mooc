BONSAI Flood Crisis Response Checklist
Pilot course: Flood Crisis Management in Estuarine Areas
Unit 3 — Coordination, Communication and Scenario Response

Use this prototype checklist when working through a flood-crisis scenario or exercise.

[ ] 1. Establish situational awareness.
Summarise what is happening now, what is changing, and what information is still uncertain.

[ ] 2. Review flood-risk and forecast information.
Consider flood-wave development, inundation forecasts, possible impacts, and how current observations compare with expectations.

[ ] 3. Check flood-defence information.
Review available dike and levee inspection information and note any issue that may change the response picture.

[ ] 4. Identify priority impacts.
Consider people, critical infrastructure, flood defences, services, and other exposed or vulnerable elements.

[ ] 5. Coordinate stakeholders.
Identify which authorities, operational teams, technical specialists, emergency services, and other stakeholders need to exchange information or act together.

[ ] 6. Communicate clearly.
State the current situation, key uncertainties, decisions, responsibilities, and next update in language appropriate to the audience.

[ ] 7. Select and justify response actions.
Choose actions that fit the evolving situation and explain how they address the most important risks or impacts.

[ ] 8. Review and adapt.
Update the situation picture as forecasts, observations, inspections, or impacts change, and revise actions when needed.

[ ] 9. Connect response to recovery and resilience.
After the immediate crisis, identify lessons that can improve future preparedness, coordination, forecasting, and long-term flood resilience.

Prototype note: This checklist is sample MOOC content, not an official BONSAI emergency procedure.
